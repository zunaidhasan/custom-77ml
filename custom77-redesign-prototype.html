Materials & Craft

Pure materials.
Honest techniques.

Custom 77 blends metalwork with woodwork to create sleek, modern furniture with a rustic touch. The goal is simple: pieces that are not just beautiful, but built to last — classic yet innovative.

  • — Solid hardwood (no veneers or particle board)
  • — Raw or powder-coated steel frames and details
  • — Durable, honest finishes that age well
  • — Joinery and welding done to last decades

How It Works

From conversation to finished piece

01

Conversation

Tell us about the space, the function, and any ideas you already have. No pressure — just a clear starting point.

02

Design & Measure

We refine the concept, confirm dimensions, and lock in materials and finishes that suit the piece and the room.

03

Build

Fabrication happens in the shop. Solid wood, clean welds, careful finishing. Progress updates along the way.

04

Deliver

Local delivery and install available in the Denver area. Distant projects ship carefully packed and tracked.

The Maker

I’m the maker behind Custom 77. With more than 12 years of experience in metal fabrication and woodworking, I started this shop because I believe furniture should be built to last — durable, timeless, affordable, and designed with purpose.

Every piece leaves the shop under my name. That means no production shortcuts and a direct line of communication from the first conversation to final delivery.
